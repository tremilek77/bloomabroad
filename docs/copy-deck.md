## How to read this

- **What changed:** length and structure only. Every claim, price, name and rule comes from the brief.
  Nothing new is invented. Gaps are marked `[NEED: ...]`.
- **P0 legal pages are not rewritten.** Privacy, Terms, Cookie Notice and Complaints are approved
  legal text, and a solicitor reviews them before payments. They get layout help only (section 9).
- **Line format:** `Title`, `Meta` and `Answer` lines are length-checked by `scripts/check-copy.mjs`
  (titles 50-60 characters, meta 150-160, answer blocks 40-60 words).
- **Wording rules kept:** assessment is never "free" (only the checklist is), no guarantee or
  visa-promise language, no "AI-driven", "visa" only in guides, disclaimers and safety lines,
  university features in the future tense, LEO and TechFoundHer never named.

## 1. Writing system (applies to every page)

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| One idea per card, one line per idea (max 18 words) | Cards are scanned, not read |
| Every page opens with a 40-60 word **answer block** that names BloomAbroad, who it is for and what it does | Lifted whole by AI assistants and search snippets (ai-seo) |
| H2s phrased as the question a visitor would type | Matches how people query and how assistants pull passages |
| Max 4 steps, max 3 cards per row, FAQs show 5 and fold the rest | Reduces scroll depth without deleting content |
| Short disclaimer under every hero, standard on `/pricing` and `/universities` | Brief rule (Fix 9) |
| No em dashes in headlines or buttons; no "not X, it's Y" lines | copywriting skill: the usual AI tells |
| Dates on anything that changes (prices, prices reviewed, guides) | AI engines favour dated, current pages |

**Findings in the brief to fix before copy goes live**

1. Homepage hero "The right student, in the right place" reads as placement or recruitment language.
   That contradicts the "no agent language" rule. Replaced below.
2. `/students` card "Spend wisely" names **visa fees**, and the outcome blurb mentions **a refusal on your record**.
   Both break the "visa only in guides, disclaimers and safety lines" rule. Reworded below.
3. `/universities` cards are in the present tense ("Every application is assessed") before the dashboard exists.
   The brief's own rule says future tense. Fixed below.
4. "Reduce time-intensive manual screening" and "stronger conversion to enrolment" are outcome claims
   with no pilot evidence yet. Softened to "is designed to" until a pilot supports them. `[NEED: pilot data]`
5. Parent FAQ says free advice "is usually paid for by universities through commission." That is a
   claim about other firms. Softened to "often". `[NEED: Remi to confirm]`
6. University FAQ says consultant review will later "focus on the cases where it adds most value."
   Privacy section 4 and Terms section 2 promise a consultant reviews **every** result. If that ever
   narrows, both legal pages change first.

## 12. SEO and AEO layer (every page)

**On-page rules (seo-audit)**
- One H1 per page, no skipped heading levels, primary phrase in the H1 and in the first 100 words
- Unique title (50-60 characters) and meta description (150-160), self-canonical, lowercase hyphenated URLs
- Every important page within 3 clicks of the homepage, descriptive link text, no orphan pages
- Core Web Vitals: LCP under 2.5s, INP under 200ms, CLS under 0.1 (the Vite build is already light)
- Sitemap lists only indexable canonical URLs. `/old`, `/admin`, `/confirm` stay out

**Schema (schema skill, JSON-LD in one `@graph` per page, must match visible text)**

| Page | Types |
|---|---|
| All | `Organization` (legal name Ubisphere Ltd, trading name BloomAbroad, `sameAs` for existing socials only), `WebSite`, `BreadcrumbList` |
| `/about` | `Person` for Remi and Olaniyi (jobTitle, alumniOf, affiliation) |
| `/pricing` | `Offer` entries with price and currency, `priceValidUntil` only when real |
| `/students`, `/universities` | `FAQPage` (visible questions only) |
| Guides | `Article` with author, `datePublished`, `dateModified` |

Honest expectations: Google dropped FAQ rich results on 7 May 2026, and the ai-seo skill states no study
isolates a schema lift on AI citations. FAQ schema is cheap semantic markup. The citation value comes from
answer-first copy, named authors, dates and original data, not the markup.

**AEO setup**
- Entity consistency: always "BloomAbroad, a trading name of Ubisphere Ltd, an Irish company in Cork"
- Allow search and user crawlers (OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Claude-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-User) in `robots.txt` with a single `User-agent: *` rule. Training crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot) are Remi's call
- Publish `/llms.txt` (what BloomAbroad is, who it is for, key links, prices, last updated)
- Keep proof (stats, quotes, the eight institutions) as HTML text, never inside images
- Monthly check: ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode ten target questions and log whether BloomAbroad is cited

**Search Console:** set up now (domain property by DNS). The brief says to confirm phrases in Search Console,
and a new site has no data for weeks, so start the clock today.
